Tension Bot

Generating climbs on a Tension Board with a small GPT.

September 2026

Tension Bot generates boulder problems for the Tension Board 2. Pick a board angle and a grade, click Generate, and it draws a new climb over a photo of the board so you can set it by looking at the screen.

Try it here. It runs on a free tier, so the first load after it has been idle can take up to a minute.

How it works

Behind the page is a small GPT I wrote from scratch: a decoder-only transformer with 978k parameters (4 blocks, 4 heads, width 128), trained on a CPU. The training data is about 15.5k community climbs for the TB2 Mirror layout, each paired with the angle it was climbed at and its community grade.

A climb is written as a sequence of hold and role tokens, where the role is start, hand, finish or foot: 

[BOS, p466, r8, p477, r6, ..., EOS]

The angle and grade you pick go in at the front of the sequence, and the model writes the rest one token at a time. The rules of the board are enforced while it writes rather than left to the model: holds and roles alternate, no hold is used twice, and a climb has at most two starts and one finish.

The server then generates up to 10 candidates and returns the first one where no start or hand hold sits above the finish.

What to expect

Most of the training data is at 40–45° and roughly V2–V7, so that is where the climbs are best. Other angles and grades are offered, but the model has seen little data there.

The model also knows nothing about hold type or orientation. It only learns which holds tend to appear together, so it can produce a climb that is unclimbable because a hold faces the wrong way.

Code

The web app is at damonseto/TBgenerator, and the model, data pipeline and training notebooks are at damonseto/tensionboardproject. The project was inspired by KilterGPT.
